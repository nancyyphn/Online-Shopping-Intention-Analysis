Visitor Type & Purchase Behavior

Comparing visitor-type composition between purchasing and non-purchasing sessions.

Dataset: Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention
Segment field: VisitorType
Prepared for: Stakeholder Review

Executive Summary

Returning Visitors dominate both purchasing and non-purchasing sessions, making them the site's largest traffic and revenue base. However, New Visitors nearly double their share when a purchase occurs — from 12% to 22% — indicating they convert disproportionately well relative to how rarely they appear in the overall traffic mix. The "Other" category remains statistically negligible in both groups.

Key Insights

01
Returning Visitors generate the majority of purchases in absolute terms.

At 77% of all purchasing sessions, Returning Visitors remain the primary revenue-driving segment — any conversion strategy still needs to center on this group's experience.

02
New Visitors convert at a disproportionately higher rate per session.

Their share nearly doubles between outcomes (12% → 22%), meaning a New Visitor session is meaningfully more likely to end in a purchase than their overall traffic share would predict.

03
Returning Visitors under-convert relative to their traffic volume.

Their share drops 10 percentage points from non-purchase to purchase sessions (87% → 77%) — a signal of missed revenue given how large this segment already is.

04
"Other" visitor type is not a meaningful segment for strategy.

Holding steady at 1% in both groups, this category is too small to influence business decisions and can be excluded from segmentation-driven initiatives.

05
Visitor type is a genuine conversion-intent signal.

The composition shift between outcomes confirms VisitorType carries predictive value and is a strong candidate feature for conversion models or real-time targeting rules.

Actionable Recommendations

RECOMMENDATION 01

Double down on first-visit conversion: New Visitors already convert well — targeted first-session incentives (welcome discounts, guided product discovery) could compound an already-strong effect.

RECOMMENDATION 02

Investigate Returning Visitor drop-off: with the largest traffic base but a lower relative conversion share, diagnose friction points — retargeting fatigue, stale offers, or cart abandonment among repeat visitors.

RECOMMENDATION 03

Add VisitorType as a segmentation feature in personalization and predictive-conversion models, since it meaningfully shifts between purchase outcomes.
